The idea

Step over the water, under the chandeliers, into a forest that happens to serve dinner. Nothing here is hurried.

The name

Antarvan (अंतर्वन) means the forest within. It is the idea behind the glass house: a quiet green world you step into, away from the street.

01   Slow dining

K's Antarvan is run on a slow dining philosophy. Order, linger and let each course arrive in its own time, in a room built to make you stay.
